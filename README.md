# 🎒 Backpack — Visa Eligibility Engine for Remote Workers

**Live site:** https://alirezatomama.github.io/backpack/

Backpack helps remote workers and digital nomads figure out **where in the world their income can legally take them** — without $3,000 consultants or contradictory Reddit threads.

Answer a few questions about how you work and earn, and Backpack checks the real 2026 rules of **19 destinations**, ranks your matches, and hands you a document checklist and a week-by-week relocation plan.

## ✨ What it does

- **Live income engine** — drag a slider to your monthly income and instantly see which destinations unlock
- **Eligibility wizard** — work type (remote employee / freelancer / founder), income stability, savings, passport, family setup, documents, refusal history and more
- **Ranked results** — match score per destination, with your top 3 compared side by side
- **Country guides** — Spain, Portugal, Georgia, Thailand, Mexico, Colombia and more, with honest "watch out" notes
- **Family-aware** — applies each program's real dependent surcharges (e.g. Portugal +50%/+30%, Costa Rica flat $5,000)
- **Relocation Blueprint** *(demo checkout)* — budget, 183-day tax calendar, letter templates, risks and a Plan B

Every threshold carries its effective date and is re-checked monthly — last check **August 2026**.

## 🛠️ How it's built

- Single self-contained `index.html` (no build step, no server) — React under the hood
- Fully responsive, works offline after first load
- Hosted free on **GitHub Pages**
- Privacy-friendly analytics via GoatCounter (no cookies)
